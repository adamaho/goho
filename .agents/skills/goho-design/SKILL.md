# Goho design

Design handoff: October 22, 2026 section navigation (title menu with title swipe) and the shared Add button, which replaces the Scan button; see [Sections and navigation](#sections-and-navigation). Builds on the October 21, 2026 item category tags for receipt lists, shared sheet headers and receipt details, updated with the Categories breakdown card and single-category example. Preserve the approved Bram, button and layout refinements below. See [Category and upload tags](#category-and-upload-tags) and the [handoff mockups](assets/mockups/README.md#item-category-tags-october-21-2026-approved-design). The written specs take precedence over older examples in the reference assets.

Goho is the family’s app. The latest design organises it into Receipts and Books sections, with more modules later; the native app implements Receipts, a Books list whose saved books open the lifted-book overlay on tap and the shared options sheet on a hold, Books search over the saved titles and authors, and scanning receipts or book covers from Add. The look is **warm, soft and friendly, with financial-app precision**: pale lavender-white in light mode, violet-charcoal in dark mode, one lavender accent, generous rounded corners, Geist type with restrained weights and proportional figures, grouped cards, and buttons that press down with a small spring.

Think "Family wallet's warmth and motion, Linear's calm surfaces", never "Material starter template".

## Bram refinements

Labelled actions and round `IconButton` controls share the same surface and palette. Back and overflow use variant 2/Grey; photo close uses that finish under the dark theme while retaining its visual fade and accessible target. The button API separates numbered finish (`variant = 1` glossy, `variant = 2` frosted/rimmed) from `color` (`Purple`, `Red`, `Grey`) and `size` (`Regular` full width, or `Small`: 44dp, hugging its label, for in-content actions such as Clear search). Existing buttons use glossy deep-periwinkle primary fills with white content, frosted light secondary fills and dark charcoal secondary fills with a continuous grey rim meeting the face directly; glossy actions retain bright upper rims. In light mode, glossy actions use tighter, quieter shadows and grey faces are brighter and nearly opaque to keep stacked actions distinct; see `references/tokens.md`. The app uses five Bram illustrations from `assets/illustrations/`: no receipts, no books, the calm thumbs-up for the empty Needs attention filter, a gentle shrug for list load errors, and the magnifying glass for a book search with no matches. Neutral Bram assets remain identity references only. Render the supplied artwork unchanged. All five status states render directly on the screen background, without an enclosing card fill, border or rounded clipping. Preserve their content padding and placement, with a 4dp gap between each illustration slot and its title. The no-receipts state uses one transparent image of Bram cradling a blank receipt in both themes. The empty Needs attention state uses one transparent image of Bram giving a calm thumbs-up in both themes, with “All good” and “Nothing needs your attention right now.” Keep his familiar lilac and cream palette, soft smile and rounded arm; no confetti or streamers. The load-error state uses one transparent image of Bram gently shrugging with his original rounded arms, without thumbs, in both themes. Pair it with “A little hiccup”, “We couldn’t load your receipts. Let’s try again.”, and the existing retry action. Books reuses the thumbs-up and shrug states, with “We couldn’t load your books. Let’s try again.” for its load error; its empty state uses the approved book-holding Bram with “No books yet” and “Saved books will appear here.” The search artwork below ships with book search; other character poses and accessories are deferred. Interactive accents are lavender. The Receipts header is text-only in every state, with the title at the 20dp text inset in a row at least 44dp tall. Buttons have 20dp corners (16dp at the small size); the Add button is fully round. Initial receipt-list and detail loading use the existing components with shared skeleton masks after 200ms of actual pending work. Do not maintain separate skeleton-only layouts. Retry buttons retain their delayed loading icon; quick requests show no skeleton shimmer or icon. Keep loaded-list background refreshes quiet. Status entrance, loading, retry, recovery, and large-text behavior are specified in `references/screens.md` section 6.

## Category and upload tags

Use the shared `Tag` for receipt upload states and item categories. Labelled tags require an icon and visible label; tags have no click handlers or editor. Tags are rectangular with 4dp corners, a muted colour fill, 14dp outline icon and 12/16sp Medium label. Categories belong to receipt items, so a receipt can have several. Approved October 21, 2026: processed list rows and the shared bottom-sheet header show the category with the highest item spend as a labelled `Tag`, followed by a neutral Grey `TagCount` (“+2”) when the receipt has more categories. Receipt details show a `CategoryBreakdown` card between the photo and Items on every receipt with items, including single-category receipts. Its category icons, names, amounts and share bars form a legend for the item icons. Keep items in receipt order and give each item a 24dp icon-only `CategoryIcon` before its name. There is no Category row in Details. Use the server’s `icon` and `color` keys for every category; see [references/components.md](references/components.md) for the ranking, mapping and accessibility rules. The bottom-sheet header reuses the same `ReceiptSummary` layout, typography, thumbnail, date, amount and category or upload tags; keep it mounted through delete confirmation. Processing and failure tags replace the old status pills, including the failed-upload sheet and scan-preview status copy. See [references/components.md](references/components.md) for the API and layout, [references/tokens.md](references/tokens.md) for all nine colour pairs, and [assets/icons/tags/README.md](assets/icons/tags/README.md) for icon sources and attribution.

## Sections and navigation

Approved design, October 22, 2026. The Add button with Scan receipt and Scan book, the title menu and switching between Receipts and a Books list with the lifted-book overlay are implemented. Delete book is implemented for saved books, from the overlay and from the options sheet opened by holding a saved row; Delete scan is implemented for failed book scans from the same sheet. Title swiping remains planned. Goho is organised into **sections**, peers at the top level: Receipts and Books, with more family modules later. There is still no bottom navigation. An ordered `Section` enum (Receipts, then Books) drives the menu; `SectionScreen` is the shared root that keeps the title, menu and Add mounted while the section content changes:

- **Section title menu.** Every section screen’s title is a `SectionTitle`: the section name in `screenTitle` followed by a 18dp chevron. Tapping it opens the `SectionMenu`, a small rounded menu dropping from the title that lists every section with its icon and count, the current one tinted with `accentContainer`. The native menu selects the current section and shows each loaded count: Receipts' All count and the number of saved books. Unknown counts are omitted. Choosing the other row queues the section change until the menu finishes its 120ms exit; the outgoing title, selected row and page stay unchanged during the fade. Choosing the current one only closes it. Outside tap, Back and tapping the title also close it. Focus enters the selected row and returns to the title; the light scrim and popup fade together.
- **Title swipe (planned, not implemented).** A short vertical swipe on the title switches without opening the menu: up for the next section, down for the previous. The title rolls with the finger and the content crossfades. No wrap, no extra hint beyond the chevron, and TalkBack gets Next and Previous section actions.
- **Add button.** Every section shares the round button and menu: both actions open the same document scanner and scan preview, with copy for the chosen kind. A successful receipt upload switches to Receipts with the new row at the top of All; a successful cover upload switches to Books with the accepted scan at the top of All. Cancelling returns to the section the scan started from. The plus is 22dp inside its 60dp button. Add holds the shared pressed appearance while its menu is open; purple glossy buttons use a violet lower shade and inner edge. The screen overlay fades in over 240ms and out over 180ms. Menu actions spring in from the bottom with a 45ms stagger and dismiss in reverse order; reduced motion snaps immediately. One round `AddButton` (+) sits in the same safe-area spot on every section, replacing the Receipts Scan button. It opens the `AddMenu`, a short vertical stack of labelled actions above it: Scan book, then Scan receipt (nearest the button), in the same order on every section. Choosing a scan for another section switches to that section only after a successful upload, so its processing row is visible.

The app reopens on the last-used section. Sections keep their own filter page and scroll positions while you switch. Back on a section root does not switch sections. Details screens push over their section as today; the section title is not shown there. Full layout, motion and accessibility specs: [screens.md section 7](references/screens.md#7-section-navigation-and-add-button); components in [components.md](references/components.md#sectiontitle-and-sectionmenu); tokens in [tokens.md](references/tokens.md#navigation-and-add-menu). Mockups: `nav-*.png` in the [mockup guide](assets/mockups/README.md#section-navigation-and-add-button-october-22-2026-approved-design).

**Books** list is implemented; see [screens.md section 8](references/screens.md#8-books-list). Seeded and saved books show their cover or, without one, a small generated cloth cover in the category colour (approved October 22, 2026), one-line title, author, optional reading level and server category tag; unfinished and failed scans stay pinned first, newest first, failed ones under Needs attention; saved books follow A–Z by title, with no sort control. Empty Books shows the approved book-holding Bram. The complete Books design follows the Receipts patterns exactly: All / Needs attention filters, a scan appearing inline at the top as “Reading cover…”, failed scans kept as Not processed and listed under Needs attention, read-only details, deletion through the shared options sheet, and the same scan preview (“New book”, Upload) targeting `POST /book-uploads`, which is implemented ([screens.md section 4](references/screens.md#4-scan-preview)). Verify the current Books API behaviour when implementing further Books screens; API availability alone does not add a Books UI. Books has no manual entry and no editing. Book search is implemented ([screens.md section 10](references/screens.md#10-book-search)): the round search button at the end of the Books title row opens a search header and filters the loaded saved books by title or author without a request per keystroke. Tapping a saved book opens the lifted-book overlay (the cover flies out of the row over the frosted list; no separate details screen), including its approved Delete book with in-place confirmation and immediate dismissal once deletion is accepted, and a generated “cloth” cover (the category colour as the cloth, a darker spine, and the title and author printed on it in a same-hue contrasting colour) when a book has no cover image; the Books list uses its small version: [screens.md section 9](references/screens.md#9-book-overlay-lifted-book). Holding a saved book opens the shared options sheet with the row’s `BookSummary` header and Delete book; tapping or holding a Not processed scan opens it with Delete scan ([screens.md section 5](references/screens.md#5-receipt-and-book-options-sheet-and-delete-confirmation)). Other unbuilt Books screens have no separate spec yet; derive them from the Receipts specs and the server’s Books contract, and ask before inventing anything else.

## Geist typography

Bundle Geist Regular (400), Medium (500), and SemiBold (600). Do not use weight 700 or Geist Mono. Apply the current sizes, weights and tracking in `references/tokens.md`, keeping the approved proportional digits and fixed Receipts header.

## Non-negotiables

1. **Use the tokens.** Every color, text style, shape, spacing value and motion spec comes from `GohoTheme` (see `assets/compose/GohoTheme.kt`). No hard-coded hex values, dp values or `MaterialTheme.typography` styles in screens.
2. **One accent, used on purpose.** Deep periwinkle (`buttonPrimary`) with white content marks primary actions and the Add button. Lavender (`accent`) marks the processing state. Do not add decorative accent shapes outside the supplied artwork. Upload failure tags use the muted Orange tag palette; other attention cues retain `attention` orange. Red (`danger`) remains reserved for destructive actions. Display-only category tags are the approved exception: Red, Orange, Yellow, Green, Blue, Purple, Pink, Grey and Brown distinguish categories without implying an action or error.
3. **Use proportional numbers.** Disable tabular figures throughout the app, including amounts, dates, counts and times. Use natural digit widths and zero tracking for metadata and labels; never use a monospaced font. Format all money with the shared formatter: exactly two decimals, half-even rounding and locale grouping separators; missing amounts show “—”.
4. **Missing amounts show "—", never $0.00.** Receipts that failed processing stay in the list, marked "Not processed", and are never hidden.
5. **Processing is inline.** A new receipt appears at the top of the list immediately with a shimmering "Reading receipt…" tag with a scan icon. No blocking screens or hero scanner.
6. **No ripples.** Press feedback is the Goho press: scale to 97%, move down 1dp, shadow drops, fill darkens, spring back on release. Use `indication = null` plus the press modifier; tappable list rows use `Modifier.listRowPressFeedback`. Never a sunken or inset-shadow pressed state.
7. **Rounded, by role.** Thumbnail 8dp; primary and secondary buttons 20dp (not pills); cards and photo frame 20dp; display tags 4dp; section menu 20dp; Add button, add-menu actions, count badges, filter and icon buttons fully round.
8. **No bottom navigation.** Each section’s title opens the section menu (title swiping is planned); one shared round Add button floats at the bottom right of every section.
9. **Light and dark, following the system.** Both palettes share token names (`GohoLightColors`, `GohoDarkColors`). Never branch on theme in screens; the only exceptions are baked into the components (card edges, top highlights). The photo frame and photo viewer are dark in both themes.

## Scope: build only what the app supports

Approved design for future implementation: title swiping (see [Sections and navigation](#sections-and-navigation)). Implement these when requested; updating this skill does not implement the feature or establish backend support. The app currently supports: switching between Receipts and Books from the title menu, remembering the last section; a Books list with small generated covers, All and Needs attention filters and pull-to-refresh, where saved books open the lifted-book overlay (frost, Close, photo or large generated cover, title, author and category, shared cover flight, swipe down to close, the shared photo viewer for photo covers, and Delete book through `DELETE /books/{bookId}`), holding a saved book to open the shared options sheet with the same Delete book, tapping or holding a Not processed scan to open it with Delete scan through `DELETE /book-uploads/{uploadId}`, and queued or processing scan rows stay display-only; searching saved books by title or author from the Books header, with the magnifying-glass Bram when nothing matches ([screens.md section 10](references/screens.md#10-book-search)); scanning a book cover from the Add menu’s Scan book with the shared scan preview and `POST /book-uploads`, showing the accepted scan at the top of Books All; the receipts list with All and Needs attention filters and pull-to-refresh, inline processing status, scanning from the Add menu’s Scan receipt action, a scan preview with Upload and Cancel, and a read-only details screen for processed receipts with grouped line items and an expandable photo viewer, plus receipt deletion from the list and details through a shared options and confirmation sheet. Empty and error state designs for the list are in `references/screens.md` section 6.

**Do not build, stub or add placeholder UI for any of these** (they are not supported yet):

- Search on Receipts (reuse the book search pattern if it is requested)
- Manual book entry and book editing
- Editing receipt details, categories or notes
- Sharing or exporting
- Swipe-to-delete, multi-select deletion, and undo
- Retaking a photo
- A details screen for receipts that are processing or failed processing (failed rows open receipt options; processing rows are not interactive)
- Monthly totals, account or settings entry points, unrelated overflow actions
- Server connection status indicators

If a task seems to need one of these, stop and ask instead of inventing UI.

## Receipt deletion designs

The current handoff reuses the receipt list’s `ReceiptSummary` in the menu and confirmation: thumbnail, merchant/date on the left, and price with category or upload tags on the right. The sheet header is display-only and remains mounted while the actions change. The menu uses a plain trash icon; confirmation has only the shared receipt header and action buttons, with no title, large icon or description. Keep content transitions together without stagger or bounce. It defines a shared receipt-options and delete-confirmation sheet, opened from the details overflow, a long press on a finished row, or a tap on a Not processed row. See `references/screens.md` for the flows and `references/components.md` / `references/tokens.md` for sheets, danger colors and hold feedback. The shared implementation uses DELETE /receipts/{receiptId} for processed receipts and DELETE /receipt-uploads/{uploadId} for failed uploads. Queued and processing uploads cannot be deleted. Processed rows open the sheet on a long press; failed rows open it on tap or long press. Loaded receipt details expose the same sheet through the overflow button. Cancel stays on details; confirming deletion closes the sheet before returning to the optimistically updated list, without waiting for the server. Natively, `OptionsSheet` owns the sheet and confirmation, briefly preventing duplicate input while it hands off deletion and exits; `ReceiptOptions` and `BookOptions` supply the header and copy. Activity-owned `PendingDeletions` masks pending rows, their linked uploads and derived counts across list, search and refresh results. `DeletionFeedback` presents later failures above Add using the shared surface, body text and a small variant 2/Grey Dismiss action. A failed or ambiguous DELETE is checked with GET before the row can return; 204 or 404 confirms absence. There is no Undo, new data library, persistent queue or restart guarantee. See the shared deletion behavior in `references/screens.md` section 5 and the [current native deletion captures](assets/mockups/README.md#optimistic-deletion-native-implementation). Holding a saved book row opens `BookOptions` with the row’s `BookSummary` and Delete book, using the same `DELETE /books/{bookId}` as the book overlay. Tapping or holding a Not processed book scan opens `BookOptions` with its scan `BookSummary` and Delete scan, using `DELETE /book-uploads/{uploadId}` and the same optimistic removal, GET check and feedback; queued and processing book scans stay display-only.

## Approved receipt-list refinements

Preserve these refinements when adapting the reference code or older mockups:

- Title the list “Receipts”. Keep smaller, separate filter tabs below the title in a fixed header (selected pill, no enclosing track), with no scroll-driven resizing or movement. Keep 44dp filter tap targets, both counts, a 12dp gap below the header, and the 12dp top fade.
- Pull down at the top of the list or empty filter to refresh receipts and upload statuses together. Keep the header, selected filter, cached rows, and the Add button in place. Fill the pull ring completely and give one threshold haptic before release; keep it spinning in reserved space during manual refresh, hide on success, and morph to an orange warning triangle on failure. Keep background polling quiet. A failed refresh retains cached content and offers the standard secondary retry action. See `references/screens.md` for details.
- Use two horizontal receipt pages: All on the left and Needs attention on the right. Pages follow the finger so both are visible halfway through a drag; settle with native pager distance and velocity. Tab taps animate to the page. Keep each page’s vertical scroll position, shared data and row components, and one Add button mounted outside the pager across populated, empty, loading and error states. Preserve vertical scrolling, pull-to-refresh, and row taps/holds; do not wrap at the ends.
- After a successful upload, return to All at the top and keep it at the top when that upload arrives in refreshed data. Consume this one-time request so ordinary polling and back navigation preserve scroll positions.
- Sort each receipt day newest upload first, including successfully processed uploads. Rows without upload timestamps follow in stable server order; do not invent a timestamp for manual receipts.
- Keep receipt rows fully visible at the bottom; do not add a gradient behind the Add button. Anchor the Add button to the system-bar/display-cutout safe area, including cold launches from launcher search; the receipt list must not reserve space for an outgoing keyboard.
- Use a neutral receipt icon for missing photos, a lavender scanning receipt for processing, and an orange receipt with an attention mark for failures. These placeholders do not imply a new server field or image endpoint.
- Tappable receipt rows (Processed and Not processed) share the saved Books row feedback through `Modifier.listRowPressFeedback` (`ListComponents.kt`): the inset, rounded `surfacePressed` fill while pressed, scale 0.98 once a press becomes a hold, no ripple. Processing and skeleton rows stay inert. Keep the receipt header mounted across the options and confirmation states. Omit the “Delete this receipt?” heading; show Delete receipt and Cancel directly below the shared header. Animate the sheet content smoothly without stagger, bounce or a moving trash icon; snap when animations are disabled.
- Center the visible text bounds inside tags and filter count badges, rather than the font line box. Keep a minimum height that can grow for larger text; labelled tags wrap long labels and always keep their icon. `TagCount` is the display-only count exception; `CategoryIcon` remains fixed at 24dp.

## Approved receipt-details refinements

- Follow the updated layout: hero, photo when uploaded, Categories and Items cards when items are present, then Details. The Categories card uses the same 24dp `CategoryIcon` as each item row and remains visible for single-category receipts. Show “Items” without an item count.
- Omit the separate Currency row from Details; the code is already shown beside the hero total.
- Put Total at the bottom of Items and omit a second Total in Details. With no items, put Total in Details. Keep Subtotal and Tax in Details as requested. Details has no Category row; categories appear in the Categories card and on each item.
- Item names stay on one line at every font size, with an ellipsis for overflow. Center the name and amount vertically with the category tag. Keep the full name in the item’s accessibility announcement. Amounts use exactly two decimals and the receipt currency symbol.
- Show amounts with a prominent symbol and number followed by a smaller, subdued currency code (for example, “$46.78 CAD”), baseline aligned. Show codes only beside the 44sp details hero (20sp code) and receipt-list/shared sheet-header totals (12sp code). Use `textTertiary` for the code. All other amounts—items, item-card total, subtotal, tax, fallback details total—keep their currency symbol and omit the code. The trailing code identifies dollar currencies, so use “$”, not “CA$” or “US$”. Unknown codes have no invented symbol; missing amounts remain “—” with no code.
- Omit the entire details photo section, including its spacing, for receipts without an upload. Uploaded receipts retain the photo loading and missing states; missing uploaded photos show “No receipt photo available” with no expand control. Make the loaded photo area tappable to open the viewer, with an accessible “View receipt photo” action. Do not show a separate expand button, even where older mockups include one. See `references/screens.md` section 3 for zoom, hidden controls, swipe dismissal, transitions and rotation, and `assets/mockups/README.md` for all five viewer mockups.
- Keep receipt loading and retry states accessible, with back navigation available.

- Every screen state in `screens.md` renders in both light and dark: processing, not processed, processed, foreign currency, filtered, empty.
- Nothing from the out-of-scope list above appears anywhere.
- No Material ripple appears anywhere in Goho screens.
- Text contrast is at least 4.5:1. Use the measured pairs in `references/tokens.md`; filter counts use `textSecondary`.
- All touch targets are at least 44dp; icon-only buttons have content descriptions.
- Animations stop or become static when the system "Remove animations" setting is on.
- Edge-to-edge: content respects status bar and navigation bar insets.
