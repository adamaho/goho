# Brand: Bram

Bram is Goho's mascot and the whole brand: logo, app icon and illustrations. Goho is a family OS, so the mark is a character, not a receipt, and it has no letters.

Bram: a broad, cushion-shaped periwinkle body, two small ivory horns, big short arms, tiny feet, glossy dark oval eyes and a quiet smile, in matte soft clay. No blush, no redesign. These files are cut from the approved family concept sheet (Bram is one of six: Goh, Pip, Bram, Lumi, Nook and Tumble).

## Files

- `bram.png`: full-resolution cutout (491×328, transparent).
- `bram-mark-{64,128,256,491}.png`: the logo mark at fixed widths. In the app header use `bram-mark-128.png` at 40dp.
- `icon/ic_launcher_foreground_432.png`, `icon/ic_launcher_background_light_432.png`, `icon/ic_launcher_monochrome_432.png`: Android adaptive icon layers at xxxhdpi (108dp = 432px). Bram sits about 52dp wide inside the 66dp safe circle. Downscale for the other densities (108dp: mdpi 108, hdpi 162, xhdpi 216, xxhdpi 324).
- `icon/ic_launcher_background_deep_432.png`: alternate deep indigo background (not the default).
- `icon/app-icon-*-full-432.png`, `icon/preview-*.png`: flattened icons and launcher-mask previews (circle, squircle, themed).
- `icon/play-store-512.png`: store listing icon.

## Do and don't

- Use Bram at his approved pose and proportions. Scale him; never stretch, recolour, outline or add blush.
- Give him room: at least a quarter of his width of clear space around him.
- On dark backgrounds use him as is; his clay shading carries enough contrast.
- Pair the mark with text only as separate elements (for example, the header mark beside the "Receipts" title). There is no wordmark lockup.
